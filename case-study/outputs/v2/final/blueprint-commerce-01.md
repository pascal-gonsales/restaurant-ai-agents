## 4. Top 5 Actions (ranked by dollar impact)

- **Confidence: 4.** The amounts and the absence of an approval requirement are certain. No integrity problem is implied: the gap is a missing control, not a person.
- **How to implement:** (1) Turn on the manager-code requirement for post-send voids. (2) Reclassify DSC_02 to require approval, or cap it. (3) Review the cooked-and-unapproved events as a process, not as people. (4) Report unapproved post-send void count weekly.
