# Examples (FICTIONAL SAMPLES)

> FICTIONAL. Every file in this folder is a synthetic sample. The venues,
> people, dates, and numbers are invented to show what each deliverable looks
> like. Nothing here describes a real restaurant, a real client, or real
> financials. All figures are round and labeled (sample data). Brand
> attribution uses the runtime placeholder `{{REPORT_BRAND}}` and the
> call-to-action uses the `[CALENDLY_LINK]` placeholder.

## What these are

Three sample deliverables, one per agent team:

| File | Team | What it shows |
|------|------|---------------|
| `sample-blueprint.md` | Performance Audit | A Next Year Blueprint for a fictional bistro: executive summary, per-day operating recommendations with confidence scores, top-5 revenue actions with dollar math, cost flags, and mandatory data-quality notes. |
| `sample-money-leak-report.md` | Demo Builder | A public-data Operations Intelligence Report for a fictional brasserie: one headline number, 3 findings (theme, frequency, trend, evidence quotes, action, estimated impact), credibility line, and a single CTA. |
| `sample-variance-alert.md` | Food Cost Sentinel | A food-cost variance report for a fictional kitchen: explicit period window, summary totals, a Top-10 variance table, and a red-flags section (What / Evidence / Likely cause / Recommended action). |

## How to read these

- Treat every number as a placeholder. They are round and tagged (sample data)
  on purpose, so no one mistakes a sample for a benchmark.
- The structure is the point. These samples demonstrate the section layout,
  the confidence scoring, the source-citation habit, and the dollar-impact
  framing that the agent prompts enforce.
- `{{REPORT_BRAND}}` and `[CALENDLY_LINK]` are substituted at runtime. They are
  intentionally left as placeholders here.

## What these are NOT

- NOT real client work. No real venue, person, supplier, or financial figure
  appears anywhere in this repository.
- NOT a performance claim. There is no "tested on N clients" and no real
  revenue or labor data. The samples illustrate format, not results.
- NOT live HTML. The Demo Builder and Performance Audit agents also produce
  branded HTML deliverables at runtime; the markdown samples here show the
  content structure without shipping a rendered page.
